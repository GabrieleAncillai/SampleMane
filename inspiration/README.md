# Inspiration

Reference screenshots for the visual direction of Mane. They do not have to be
productivity apps. A bank, a music player, a game, anything with a detail worth
borrowing is welcome.

GymMane and Streak by InlitX are the primary style references for the app. Use
their screens, components, motion and typography first; use this folder for
extra references and details worth adopting.

## How to name files

`app-screen-detail.png`, for example:

- `revolut-bottom-nav.png`
- `linear-command-menu.png`
- `streak-empty-state.png`

Keep the source in the filename or add it to `sources.md`. If it comes from X,
Pinterest, Dribbble or Figma, keep the link so credit is easy later.

## What to look for

- Bottom navigation and tab structure.
- How a day or week is laid out when there is a lot on it.
- Empty states that do not feel like an error.
- A calm way to show "this is done" without crossing everything out.
- Color use for spaces (Home vs Work) without turning the app into a rainbow.
- Typography for dates and times.
